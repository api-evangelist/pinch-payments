Starting a SaaS business means building software that customers access online for a recurring fee, rather than software they install and own outright. If you are serious about starting a SaaS company this year, the path runs through four stages: validating the problem, building a lean product, choosing the right pricing model, and setting up billing infrastructure that scales before you need it to.
