Pool builders automate deposits and progress payments by connecting their accounting software to a payments platform, setting up one customer authorisation per job, and raising each construction milestone as its own invoice that collects automatically. This replaces manual follow-up at every stage and smooths the lumpy cash flow that comes with seasonal, project-based work.
