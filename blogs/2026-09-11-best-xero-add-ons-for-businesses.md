Xero covers accounting well, but most businesses need more than accounting software to run day to day. Add-ons close the gaps in payments, job management, reporting, and customer relationships. This guide covers the best Xero add-ons across each category, starting with payments, since that's where most businesses lose the most time to manual admin.
