If you have been watching the news about Australia's card surcharge changes, you are probably wondering what it means for your small business. From 1 October 2026, businesses will no longer be able to surcharge card payments. The Reserve Bank of Australia concluded that surcharging no longer works as intended and has agreed to allow card networks to enforce no-surcharge rules from that date.
